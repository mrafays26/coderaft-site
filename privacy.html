Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

In short: CodeRaft does not collect any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app doesn't send anything over the internet.

What the app stores

To remember where you are, CodeRaft saves a few things only on your iPhone, in the app's own private storage:

  • your progress: completed lessons, exercises and quizzes, XP, level and daily streak
  • the code you passed each exercise with, so you can review it later
  • the code you write in the Playground

This information never leaves your device, and we can't see it. It may be included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or computer backups), which are controlled by you and Apple.

Deleting your data

Tap Reset Progress on the Progress tab to clear your progress, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Children

CodeRaft is suitable for learners of all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds online features), we'll update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
